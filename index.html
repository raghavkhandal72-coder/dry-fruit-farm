Our Heritage

Direct From Orchards. Graded With Care.

Since 2010, Dry Fruit Farm (DFF) has bridged the gap between authentic global orchards and health-conscious Indian households.

01

Handpicked At Harvest

We source directly during prime harvesting seasons from Kashmir, California, Iran, and Chile, ensuring the highest natural oil content and crisp texture.

02

Laser Graded For Uniformity

Every kernel is sorted and laser-graded for size, color, and density. Zero shriveled pieces, zero broken fragments.

03

Lab Tested For 100% Purity

Third-party lab certified for zero pesticides, zero artificial polishes, and optimal moisture standards.

04

Nitrogen Flush Vacuum Packing

Sealed in high-barrier multilayer pouches to prevent air contact and keep nuts crunchy for months.

05

Retail Stores & Doorstep Dispatch

Supplied fresh to 20+ retail stores and shipped direct-to-consumer across India via priority courier networks.

06

Luxury Festive Gifting
